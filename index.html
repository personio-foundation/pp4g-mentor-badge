PP4G Mentor Badge Generator

Create your LinkedIn badge - fill in your details and download.

Badge is 1200 × 600 px - ideal for LinkedIn posts and "Licenses & Certifications".

How to add your badge on LinkedIn

  1. Go to your LinkedIn profile and click "Add profile section"
  2. Select "Recommended" → "Add licenses & certifications"
  3. Fill in: Name = People Partner for Good Mentor, Issuing organization = Personio Foundation, Issue date = your start date (month/year)
  4. Upload your downloaded badge image as the credential media
  5. Save and share as a post to celebrate your contribution!
